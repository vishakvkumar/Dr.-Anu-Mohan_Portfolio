# Dr. Anu Mohan — Academic & Researcher Portfolio Website

An academic and researcher portfolio website for **Dr. Anu Mohan**, Assistant Professor, Public Health Researcher, Gerontologist, and Social Scientist.

## Overview
- **Designation:** Assistant Professor, Department of Social Work, Rajagiri College of Social Sciences (Autonomous), Kochi
- **Consultancy:** Chief Research Consultant, ReDCom by Nord
- **Doctoral Focus:** Prasanna School of Public Health, Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE) — *Alternative care for older adults in migrant households of Kerala: A multi-stakeholder enquiry using mixed method approach* (Ph.D. Awarded)

- **Key Qualifications:** Ph.D. in Public Health (MAHE), MSW First Rank & Best Outgoing Student (Rajagiri), UGC-NET & JRF, Kerala SET in Social Work, B.Sc. Physics (Overall A+).
- **Trainer Portfolio:** Competitive exam training for UGC NET-JRF/SET and Kerala PSC (ICDS Supervisor, DCPO, Prison Officer); Research methodology capacity building (proposal writing, scoping/systematic reviews, data analysis in Jamovi/SPSS/R/NVivo).

## Website Structure
The website is built as a multi-page static web application with high visual clarity, pastel mint accents, responsive layouts, and accessible typography (Playfair Display & Plus Jakarta Sans).

- `index.html`: **Home & Biography** — Academic headline, hero credentials, comprehensive biography, software skills, areas of research interest, and dedicated **Training & Consultancy Portfolio**.
- `experience.html`: **Experience & Education** — Chronological academic and professional appointments (including Assistant Professor at Rajagiri, Chief Research Consultant at ReDCom by Nord, and trainer roles), education degrees, honors & awards, international exposure (UniSA Adelaide), 15 specialized credit courses, 10 workshops attended, and fieldwork projects.
- `research.html`: **Research, Publications & Presentations** — Ph.D. dissertation overview, MACS copyright card, 12 peer-reviewed publications (including the 2026 *Global Public Health* paper) with interactive tag & search filtering, 10 conference presentations with year tabs, and 14 invited lectures.
- `contact.html`: **Contact & References** — Academic and personal contact details, institutional address, ReDCom by Nord affiliation, ORCID profile, references for Dr. Lena Ashok (MAHE) and Dr. Rajeev SP (Rajagiri), and an interactive inquiry form.
- `styles.css`: Custom vanilla CSS styling with pastel mint & deep teal design tokens, responsive breakpoints, and custom squircle portrait framing.
- `script.js`: Dynamic client-side logic for sticky header shadows, active section tracking, publication instant search & category chips, presentation year tabs, and contact form handling.
- `assets/`:
  - `anu_mohan_profile.jpg`: Official portrait photograph
  - `Dr_Anu_Mohan_CV.pdf`: Curriculum Vitae
  - `rajagiri_profile.jpg`: Faculty profile asset

## Local Development
To run this website locally:

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/vishakvkumar/Dr.-Anu-Mohan_Portfolio.git

# Navigate to project directory
cd Dr.-Anu-Mohan_Portfolio

# Start a local HTTP server
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then open your browser at `http://localhost:8000`.
