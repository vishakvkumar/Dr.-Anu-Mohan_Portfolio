Research, Publications & Talks

Investigating elder support systems, healthcare access, and policy responses in demographic transition contexts.

Doctoral Research & Registered Copyright

Prasanna School of Public Health, Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE)
Dissertation Title

"Alternative care for older adults in migrant households of Kerala: A multi-stakeholder enquiry using mixed method approach"

Her doctoral enquiry investigates the critical support gaps and alternative structural configurations of elder care in Kerala—a state heavily impacted by international migration. By blending quantitative structural models with qualitative stakeholder deep-dives, this research maps the paradigm shift of care beyond co-residence.
