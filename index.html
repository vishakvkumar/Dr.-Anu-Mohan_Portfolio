Biography & Career Summary

Anu Mohan is a researcher and an educator specializing in ageing, migration, public health, and long-term care systems. She is currently serving as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Social Work at Rajagiri College of Social Sciences (Autonomous), Kochi.

She has submitted her Ph.D. from the Prasanna School of Public Health, Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE), where her doctoral research investigated alternative care arrangements for older adults in migrant households in Kerala. She is the first rank holder in the Master of Social Work (MSW) program at Rajagiri College of Social Sciences and was conferred the Roy and Chacko Memorial Award for Best Outgoing Student.

She has served as a Research Consultant at the N-SPRITE Center at the National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences (NIMHANS), Bengaluru, contributing to projects related to suicide prevention, monitoring and evaluation, and public health research.

Anu is also a recipient of the prestigious Endeavour Australia Cheung Kong Award (2019), under which she completed her international field practicum at the University of South Australia (UniSA), Adelaide. With over five years of experience in training and academic consultation, she possesses proven expertise in delivering workshops on research methodology, evidence synthesis, data analysis, and scientific writing.

She is also the copyright holder of the Measure of Alternative Care and Support (MACS), an assessment tool designed to evaluate care and support systems among community-dwelling older adults. Her work continues to contribute significantly to the fields of ageing, social care, public health, and social policy.
